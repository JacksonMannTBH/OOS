Version 1.0.4 (234). No sign-in or purchases are required. Open the map from Home. Settings > Notifications & state offers all 50 states; active aircraft and coverage vary. Live data requires internet. CHP aircraft appear in both California regions; user-disabled aircraft stay disabled.

Appearance and gauges: Settings > Ride mode & display has Color Mode, five Distance Colors palettes (swatches without names), and Swipe gauge previews for G-force, Lean arc and Moving horizon. Red/blue/green accents mute map topography to gray. Distance palettes also color Live Tracking on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and CarPlay. Help includes CarPlay instructions.

Start a ride and swipe Map or Stats to open the selected gauge; swipe again to return. Mount a physical iPhone rigidly in portrait, screen facing the rider, aligned with the bike. Tap the level/calibration icon. Calibration accepts gentle movement, averaging a short window without a GPS speed limit; abrupt or invalid readings defer it. Upright on level ground gives the best reference. Calibrating on a lean or slope offsets readings. The calibrated icon/border use the palette's Clear color. G-force: braking moves the dot up, acceleration down, left turns right, right turns left. Lean arc shows signed roll and confirmed peaks; Moving horizon shows a large signed pitch value. Live angles work with peak saving off. Resets clear their peaks. Readings are estimates; calibrate each ride. Motion sampling pauses when inactive/locked, independently of GPS recording. Motion data stays on the device.

Ride Mode: Tap MPH to expand speed; portrait puts status and nautical-mile distance below the map. Landscape puts map left and speed right. Tap the large speed circle to restore controls. The chart icon opens Stats; the map icon returns. Recording continues across views. End Ride shows route/statistics; the ten latest summaries are on the device and accessible from Home.

Background location supports two independent, explicitly started features:
1. Ride recording: Home > Start Ride, confirm if prompted, allow Precise Location. Move, switch apps or lock the phone; location updates keep measuring speed/distance. Return > End Ride stops recording and shows the summary.
2. Aircraft Live Tracking: Home > Start Live Tracking, accept prompt. While locked or in another app, location updates calculate nearby aircraft distances for the Live Activity. Home > Stop Live Tracking ends it. The Home Screen widget opens Ride Mode. Observations older than four minutes are excluded; a healthy feed with no tracked airborne aircraft shows Clear.
End Ride and Stop Live Tracking control separate sessions. When both are off, background location stops. Continuous updates measure speed/distance and changing aircraft distances as the user moves. No upgrade to Always location is requested.

Police: Tap the ride police button to report at the current precise location after acknowledging first-use guidance. Hold about 0.7 seconds to play a pig oink; release to report a shared pig marker. Nearby sightings offer Still Here/Not Seen. A newly accepted Still Here confirmation from a hold changes only its target to a pig. Other sightings keep police icons. Both maps show pigs for updated clients. Precise-location, proximity, duplicate-vote, cooldown and expiry rules still apply. iPhone sound uses media volume, including Silent mode, mixing with audio. Aircraft glide between received points without forward prediction. Paths stay visible while refreshing; helicopter blades animate independently.
